# v0.20.0 — plan archive (stages 225–226)

Students and groups get a colour: the teacher picks it in the student's and the group's dialog from a fixed palette
of ten M3 static colours (roles of both themes computed by `@material/material-color-utilities` from each colour's
Fidelity scheme, not harmonized — the colours must stay apart). Rows of the teacher's lists tied to a student or a
group show it (the avatar, the group's icon, the lesson in the calendar), and so do the chips of student and group
multiselects.
